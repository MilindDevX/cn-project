# Phase 1: caching headers (the backends answer GET only, so -i is used instead of -I)

$ curl -si https://app.team18.test/api/cache
HTTP/2 200
alt-svc: h3=":443"; ma=2592000
cache-control: max-age=60
content-type: text/plain; charset=utf-8
date: Sun, 04 Oct 2026 15:35:52 GMT
etag: "backend-a-cache-v1"
via: 1.1 Caddy
x-backend: A
content-length: 30

Cached response from Backend A

# Cache revalidation, 19 Nov 2026 (the x-edge header was added in Phase 2)
# The ETag from Backend A gives 304 on A and 200 on B, because each backend has its own ETag.

$ curl -si https://app.team18.test/api/cache
HTTP/2 200
alt-svc: h3=":443"; ma=2592000
cache-control: max-age=60
content-type: text/plain; charset=utf-8
date: Thu, 19 Nov 2026 13:20:08 GMT
etag: "backend-a-cache-v1"
via: 1.1 Caddy
x-backend: A
x-edge: primary
content-length: 30

Cached response from Backend A

$ curl -si -H 'If-None-Match: "backend-a-cache-v1"' https://app.team18.test/api/cache   # round robin sends it to B
HTTP/2 200
alt-svc: h3=":443"; ma=2592000
cache-control: max-age=60
content-type: text/plain; charset=utf-8
date: Thu, 19 Nov 2026 13:20:08 GMT
etag: "backend-b-cache-v1"
via: 1.1 Caddy
x-backend: B
x-edge: primary
content-length: 30

Cached response from Backend B

$ curl -si -H 'If-None-Match: "backend-a-cache-v1"' https://app.team18.test/api/cache   # next request goes to A
HTTP/2 304
